# Three-Brand Content Boundary

Status: canonical marketing boundary

The same research object may feed more than one public brand, but the resulting communication must preserve distinct sender intent:

- eDebatte: evidence, debate state, open questions, participation and product truth.
- VoiceOpenGov: mission, membership, community, partners and transparently authorized movement positions.
- Vote4Gov: comparative governance, institutional alternatives and clearly labelled hypotheses.

Shared research never implies shared editorial voice. Reuse happens at evidence/input level, not by copying the same political message under three logos.

Every generated marketing asset should therefore be traceable to: source/evidence, public brand, campaign, channel, CTA, review status and distribution record.
