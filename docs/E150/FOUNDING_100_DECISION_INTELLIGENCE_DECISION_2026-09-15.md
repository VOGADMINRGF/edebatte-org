# FOUNDING 100 — Decision Intelligence Product Decision

Date: 2026-09-15
Status: project-owner authorized for reviewable implementation
Scope: eDebatte professional offers, homepage acquisition, institutional pricing surface, organic search acquisition

## Decision

The project owner authorizes a limited launch program named **Founding 100** for professional eDebatte Decision Intelligence services.

The program is deliberately separate from the free public participation layer. Public facts, evidence visibility and ordinary civic participation must not become paywalled by this decision. Professional customers pay for individually commissioned research, synthesis, monitoring, workflow, delivery and service effort — not for a privileged version of truth.

## Founding 100 exception

The first 100 accepted paid professional engagements may receive a **50% Founding 100 launch price**.

This is an explicit, narrow exception to the previously documented 30% discount ceiling. The exception applies only to the Founding 100 campaign and does not change the general discount ceiling for other packages, campaigns or line items.

For one-time products, the 50% launch price applies to the first commissioned package. For recurring products, it applies only to the first month; later months return to the regular list price unless a separately documented commercial decision says otherwise.

No UI may claim an exact number of remaining Founding 100 places unless that number comes from a reliable source of truth. Until such tracking exists, public copy may say only that the offer applies while Founding 100 places remain available.

## Commercial and legal boundary

Checkout remains disabled in this slice. The offers are **inquiry / manual-offer only** until the invoice, tax, legal and payment setup is reviewed and activated separately.

Public wording must not describe payments for these commissioned services as donations, charitable gifts or tax-deductible contributions. The transparent founder-stage wording may state that eDebatte is still being built independently, that professional services in this early phase are offered directly by the founder, and that revenues from these services are used to continue building eDebatte and VoiceOpenGov.

The customer commissions a concrete professional service. This decision does not itself determine the final legal entity, tax treatment, invoice wording or payment-provider implementation.

## Product promise boundary

Decision Intelligence may promise structured professional work around agreed topics, including combinations of:

- research and source collection,
- evidence and position mapping,
- historical and policy context,
- alternatives and decision options,
- impact and uncertainty framing,
- recurring topic updates at an agreed cadence,
- team-ready summaries and exports where actually available.

It must not promise real-time monitoring, guaranteed turnaround times, autonomous fact truth, guaranteed completeness or other capabilities that are not backed by current operational evidence.

## Brand boundary

The citizen-first homepage remains primary. Decision Intelligence is a secondary professional path below the public participation proposition.

The core message is:

> Fakten bleiben Fakten. Bezahlt wird für individuelle Recherche, Aufbereitung, Monitoring, Workflow und Geschwindigkeit.

The funding/transparency message is:

> Professionelle Aufträge helfen, den unabhängigen Aufbau von eDebatte und VoiceOpenGov zu finanzieren.

## Search acquisition boundary

The launch may use organic search acquisition, structured data, internal linking, high-quality topic pages and free webmaster/indexing tools. It must not call paid search advertising "free SEA". Any future Google Ads, Microsoft Ads or comparable paid placement requires a separate budget decision.

Thin programmatic pages, keyword stuffing, doorway pages, fake urgency, purchased backlinks and unverifiable search-volume claims are out of scope.

## Implementation gate

This decision authorizes a reviewable implementation branch and PR for the package configuration, homepage section, institutional Decision Intelligence landing page, structured data, sitemap exposure, tests and organic-search runbook.

It does **not** authorize merge if repository governance tests or the canonical `OpenTasks.md` state require an additional gate. Any remaining SSOT synchronization must be visible in the PR rather than bypassed.
