# eDebatte — Founding 100 Organic Search Acquisition

Date: 2026-09-15
Mode: zero paid-media budget
Goal: generate qualified professional Decision Intelligence inquiries without paid search advertising

## 1. Naming the channel correctly

SEA is paid search advertising and is therefore not literally free. This launch uses **organic search acquisition + free search-engine discovery tools + founder-led distribution**.

The operating idea is still SEA-like in one useful sense: capture high-intent search demand with a landing page that directly matches the query and offers a concrete next step.

## 2. Conversion page

Canonical commercial page:

`/pricing/institutionen/decision-intelligence`

Primary conversion:

`/kontakt?anfrage=decision-intelligence&programm=founding-100`

The page targets professional users who already have an information or decision problem. It must remain secondary to the citizen-first homepage proposition.

## 4. Content engine: T0 becomes acquisition evidence

The strongest organic asset is not a generic SEO article. It is a real, useful public decision dossier produced from the same evidence discipline that powers the professional offer.

A publishable topic page should contain, where evidence supports it:

1. the concrete question,
2. current status and chronology,
3. relevant facts and sources,
4. clearly separated positions and interests,
5. credible alternatives,
6. potential impacts and trade-offs,
7. international comparisons where relevant,
8. uncertainties and open questions,
9. source/provenance visibility,
10. a contextual professional CTA.

The CTA should say, in substance:

> Sie benötigen diese Analyse für Ihr Unternehmen, Ihren Verband oder Ihre Redaktion? eDebatte erstellt individuelle Decision Dossiers und laufende Topic Intelligence.

No public dossier should exist solely to capture a keyword. A page enters the index only if it is independently useful to a reader.

## 5. First topic backlog

Select topics from real T0 acceptance material and current public demand. Prefer topics with a clear decision question and multiple credible policy paths.

Initial patterns:

- Rentenreform: Entwicklung, Finanzierungswege und internationale Alternativen
- Pflegeversicherung: Finanzierung, Leistungsmodelle und Reformoptionen
- Krankenhausreform: Ziele, Umsetzung, Auswirkungen und Alternativen
- Wehrpflicht / Dienstmodelle: Varianten und internationale Modelle
- Energie- und Industriestandort: Kosten, Versorgung, Wettbewerbsfähigkeit and policy options
- EU AI / digital regulation: implications for companies and institutions

Before publication, each topic must pass the normal evidence and editorial gates. Do not manufacture public pages merely because a keyword looks attractive.

## 6. On-page standard

Every commercial or dossier page should have:

- one clear search-intent-aligned H1,
- unique title and meta description,
- a canonical URL,
- crawlable internal links,
- useful visible copy that matches structured data,
- source and update information where relevant,
- descriptive headings instead of keyword repetition,
- an explicit next step,
- mobile-readable layout,
- no hidden SEO text.

The Decision Intelligence page additionally uses `Service`, `OfferCatalog` and `FAQPage` structured data that reflects visible page content.

## 7. Discovery / indexing — free actions

### Google

- verify the production domain in Google Search Console,
- submit the canonical sitemap,
- inspect the Decision Intelligence URL after deployment,
- request indexing for the launch page and first flagship dossiers where appropriate,
- monitor coverage, search queries, impressions, clicks and indexing errors.

### Bing / Copilot discovery

- verify the site in Bing Webmaster Tools,
- submit/import the sitemap,
- use IndexNow when the production deployment supports a verified key and reliable change notifications,
- monitor crawl/indexing issues.

Sitemap submission improves discovery; it is not a ranking guarantee.

## 8. Internal linking

Required launch links:

- homepage professional section -> Decision Intelligence landing page,
- Decision Intelligence landing page -> institutional pricing overview,
- Decision Intelligence landing page -> contact inquiry,
- future public dossiers -> Decision Intelligence only where the professional use case is contextually relevant.

Future improvement: add a Decision Intelligence entry from the canonical institutional pricing page once that page's current governance slice is safely editable.

## 9. Founder-led zero-budget distribution

Every strong public dossier can be distributed in four forms without buying ads:

1. a LinkedIn post that leads with the decision conflict, not the product,
2. a short visual / video summary that links to the source dossier,
3. a direct, highly targeted note to relevant associations, journalists, companies or NGOs for whom the specific dossier is useful,
4. a follow-up post when the evidence or policy situation materially changes.

Do not mass-post identical promotional comments. Distribution should add topic-specific value and point to the underlying evidence page.

## 10. 30-day launch sequence

### Days 0–3

- deploy Decision Intelligence page,
- verify canonical, robots and sitemap behavior,
- validate structured data,
- submit sitemap in free webmaster tools,
- publish one flagship Decision Dossier suitable as a product demonstration.

### Days 4–10

- publish a second flagship dossier,
- link both dossiers to the professional offer contextually,
- publish founder-led LinkedIn summaries,
- contact a small list of highly relevant organisations for each dossier.

### Days 11–20

- add two to four evidence-rich topic updates/pages based on real T0 output,
- inspect Search Console/Bing queries,
- improve titles, descriptions and internal links based on observed wording,
- turn qualified questions from prospects into FAQ improvements.

### Days 21–30

- identify the pages producing qualified impressions and inquiries,
- expand only the topic clusters that show real engagement,
- stop or consolidate weak/duplicative pages,
- publish a transparent Founding 100 progress update only if the count is backed by a reliable source of truth.

## 11. Measurement

Primary business metrics:

- qualified Decision Intelligence inquiries,
- inquiry-to-offer rate,
- offer-to-paid-engagement rate,
- revenue by package,
- recurring conversion after the Founding first month.

Search metrics:

- indexed canonical pages,
- non-brand impressions,
- non-brand clicks,
- CTR on high-intent queries,
- queries that lead to the commercial landing page,
- public-dossier -> professional-page click-through.

Do not optimize for raw page count or vanity traffic.

## 12. Hard anti-spam rules

Do not use:

- doorway pages,
- city-by-city or keyword-by-keyword near-duplicates,
- mass AI pages with no independent reader value,
- hidden keyword blocks,
- fake reviews or fake scarcity,
- purchased backlink packages,
- misleading redirects,
- scraped content without substantial original value.

These constraints follow the current search-engine emphasis on useful, discoverable, well-structured content rather than scaled pages created primarily to manipulate rankings.
